# 1. Executive Summary

Customer-facing organisations receive large volumes of requests through
email, web forms, chat, mobile applications, contact centres and service
portals.

The operational challenge is not simply receiving these requests.

It is deciding, quickly and consistently:

- what the customer needs;
- how important the request is;
- whether it contains risk or vulnerability indicators;
- which team should handle it;
- which organisational knowledge or policy applies;
- what response or action should be considered;
- whether the available evidence is sufficient;
- and whether AI is permitted to act or a human must intervene.

These decisions are often made across fragmented systems, queues,
knowledge bases and operating procedures.

A routine document request may enter the same queue as a suspected fraud
report.

A calmly written message may represent significantly greater risk than an
angry complaint.

A request may be correctly classified but incorrectly prioritised.

An employee may understand the issue but still need to search several
knowledge sources before determining the correct procedure.

Generative AI can help interpret these requests, retrieve information and
generate recommendations.

However, introducing generative AI creates another operational problem:

> A plausible AI response is not necessarily a correct, evidenced or
> authorised decision.

Sigvora is designed around this gap.

**Sigvora is a Trustworthy AI customer-request intelligence platform that
helps organisations understand, prioritise, route and safely act on
customer requests using evidence-grounded AI and explicit decision
controls.**

The intended workflow is:

```text
Customer Request
       ↓
Understand
       ↓
Detect Important Signals
       ↓
Assess Risk & Priority
       ↓
Retrieve Relevant Knowledge
       ↓
Generate Evidence-Grounded Recommendation
       ↓
Determine Routing / Next Action
       ↓
Apply Governance
       ↓
Human Review or Controlled Automation
       ↓
Track SLA, Outcome and Audit Evidence
